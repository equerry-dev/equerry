Equerry is a privacy-first digital assistant you can set as your phone's default — but instead of routing everything to a single vendor's cloud, you bring your own AI backends and wire each job to the provider you choose.

Privacy is the product, enforced in code:

* No accounts, no sign-up, no telemetry, no analytics.
* Your data flows only to the providers you configure — nowhere else.
* Fully open source under the GPL-3.0. Every fork stays open.

Bring your own backend:

* Configure provider profiles for Ollama (self-hosted), Anthropic, OpenAI-compatible endpoints, and OpenRouter.
* Your API keys are stored encrypted on-device (Android Keystore) and are never logged or placed in URLs.

Per-job capability slots:

* Route Chat, Vision, Speech-to-Text, and Text-to-Speech to different providers, so you can split jobs across backends for cost, privacy, and quality.
* Use a local model for some jobs and a hosted one for others — your call.

What it does:

* Set Equerry as your system default assistant and invoke it with the assist gesture.
* Hold a spoken question-and-answer conversation end to end.
* Ask about what's on your screen — read only through Android's sanctioned Assist API, never an accessibility service.
* Ask it to set a timer or alarm, or to draft a calendar event, email, or SMS. Equerry only stages these actions; nothing is sent or saved until you confirm with a tap.
